Privacy Policy
Summary

Meimo respects your privacy. Notes you write are stored on your own device. We do not operate a server that stores the content of your notes.

Data stored on your device

Notes, images, music, theme preferences, fonts you install, and your AI API key (if provided) are stored locally on your device. This data is not uploaded to Meimo servers.

AI features

If you enable AI Features and enter your own Gemini API key, text requests are sent directly from your device to Google (Gemini) using that key. Meimo does not store or forward your API key to our servers. Use of Google services is subject to Google’s privacy policy.

Backup & import

Backup files you download (for example .zip or .meimo) remain under your control. We do not receive a copy of those files unless you share them yourself outside the app.

Analytics & ads

Meimo does not show ads and does not collect user-behavior analytics for advertising.

Policy changes

This policy may be updated as the app evolves. The latest version is always available on this page.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the contributor via the links on the About page.
