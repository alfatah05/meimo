Meimo

A beautiful personal notes app, rich in features, stored safely on your device.

App description

Meimo is a personal notes app you can install on your phone or computer. All notes are stored on your device — not sent to our servers — so your content stays private and fully under your control.

The editor supports modern text formatting, images, scenes, music, voice recording, and AI assistance. You can also change themes, fonts, back up data, and organize notes with archive or trash.

Main features
Rich text editor: bold, italic, underline, headings, lists, quotes, dividers, links, text color & highlight, plus custom fonts.
Images, scenes & music: insert images, colored scene blocks, and background music per note section.
Voice to text: record speech and have it written into a new note.
AI assistant: write or edit note sections with Gemini — using your own API key from the AI Features page.
Themes & fonts: five display themes (Light, Dark, Sepia, Paper, OLED) and a font library you can download or upload.
Backup & import: download all notes (with images/music) as a backup file, or import them again anytime.
Archive & trash: archive notes you open less often, or restore from trash before permanent deletion.
Installable on your device: add to your phone or computer home screen so it feels like a regular app, with its own icon.
Works offline: after opening once online, notes and most features stay available offline. AI features need a connection.
